# GRC Frameworks & Compliance Projects

A collection of governance, risk, and compliance work — control mappings, policy writing, and risk assessment practice using real frameworks.

## Why I built this
GRC gets treated like paperwork, but the hard part is actually understanding *why* a control exists and whether it's realistic for an organization to follow. I wanted a place to practice mapping controls, writing policy in plain language, and thinking through risk the way an auditor or a security team actually would.

## Contents

### 1. `nist-csf-mapping/`
A control mapping exercise against NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 for a fictional mid-size company. Covers Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover, with current-state vs target-state notes for each subcategory.

### 2. `iso27001-gap-analysis/`
Gap analysis against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls. Includes a maturity scoring model (0–4 scale) and prioritized remediation recommendations.

### 3. `soc2-control-matrix/`
Control matrix mapped to SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (Security, Availability, Confidentiality). Built as if preparing a company for a Type I audit.

### 4. `sample-policies/`
Written policy documents: Acceptable Use Policy, Access Control Policy, Incident Response Policy, Vendor Risk Management Policy. Written to actually be readable by non-security staff, not just legal boilerplate.

### 5. `risk-register-template.xlsx`
A working risk register template with likelihood/impact scoring, treatment plans, and owner tracking.

## Tools/references used
NIST CSF 2.0, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, AICPA TSC (SOC 2), risk scoring methodology adapted from FAIR-lite thinking.

## Notes
All company names, data, and scenarios in this repo are fictional and built for demonstration purposes.
