# Access Control Policy (Sample)

**Status:** Draft for demonstration purposes
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how access to company systems and data is granted, reviewed, and revoked, to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

## 2. Principles
- **Least privilege** — users get the minimum access needed to do their job, nothing more.
- **Need to know** — access to sensitive data is limited to roles that require it.
- **Segregation of duties** — no single person should be able to both request and approve their own access changes.

## 3. Access provisioning
- New access requests go through the employee's manager and are approved by the system/data owner.
- Access is mapped to role-based groups wherever possible instead of one-off individual grants.
- All privileged (admin) access requires a documented business justification.

## 4. Access review
- Standard user access is reviewed quarterly.
- Privileged access is reviewed monthly.
- Any access not re-certified within the review window is automatically flagged for removal.

## 5. Offboarding
- Access must be revoked within 24 hours of termination or role change.
- IT and HR maintain a shared offboarding checklist to prevent orphaned accounts.

## 6. Exceptions
Any deviation from this policy must be documented, risk-assessed, and approved by the security team, with a defined expiration date.

## 7. Related controls
- NIST CSF 2.0: PR.AA-01, PR.AA-05
- ISO 27001 Annex A: A.5.15, A.5.18
- SOC 2: CC6.1, CC6.2
